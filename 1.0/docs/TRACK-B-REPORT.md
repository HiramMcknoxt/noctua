## Choices made

1. **TLS exposure (INSTALL.md §5):** localhost Janus + local TLS-terminating reverse proxy on the office LAN, per the INTERFACE.md either/or. Rejected per-user SSH tunnels (invites shared-account workarounds) and direct Janus exposure (keeps TLS out of the stdlib service).
2. **Retention clock integrity (NETWORK.md §5):** the 14-day cap runs from the earlier of camera/server timestamps, so a mis-set camera clock cannot extend data life; skew >5 s flags rather than silently trusting either clock.
3. **Buffer policy (NETWORK.md §6):** buffered events that would age past 14 days delete at the edge unsent. Completeness loses to the retention promise, decided in advance and repeated in CONTINGENCIES A3 as an accepted cost.
4. **Defunding shutdown order (CONTINGENCIES D3):** capture stops first, evidence lifecycle completes anyway, log/outcomes pass to Clerk custody. Evidence is explicitly never collateral damage of a budget vote.

## Flagged — needs a decision or another track's attention

1. **Edge Frigate is an addition to v0.9 packaging, not a description of it.** Store-and-forward on LTE/PtP sites requires a small Frigate edge instance (or equivalent buffer) *at the site*; the v0.9 compose runs Frigate centrally only. INTERFACE.md's store-and-forward paragraph implies the edge buffer, and NETWORK.md documents it as the design — but Track A / packaging must add the per-site edge unit to the BOM and the Frigate bridge must support deferred ingest. Cost impact is inside the Clark County off-grid site estimate, but the *software* work is real and unscoped. **This is the largest gap between the documents and the shipped v0.9 code.**
2. **Frigate media vs. "raw video is not retained."** Spec v2 §3 says raw video is not a function of the system; Frigate nonetheless keeps event media under its ≤14-day media retention (v0.9 config). NETWORK/INSTALL size a media buffer accordingly and treat media as inside the retention promise. If the Commission intends *zero* video artifacts (crops only), Frigate must be configured record-off/snapshots-only and the sizing shrinks — a Commission/policy decision, flagged rather than resolved here.
3. **All per-read byte sizes are assumptions.** Crop ~100 KB, metadata ~2 KB, full-frame ~400 KB drive the bandwidth and storage math. They are labeled everywhere they are used, but they remain unmeasured until Stage 0. Spec v2 §12 also cautions that Cabot read *volume* at pilot sites is unmeasured; the 4,936/camera/day figure used here is the INTERFACE.md constant from CPD's reported 9-camera totals, applied as instructed — site-level rates will differ by site.
4. **Checkpoint key custody is documented, not decided.** Options (a)/(b)/(c) in INSTALL.md §6 have materially different insider guarantees; CANT-DO.md's insider sentence is written against the default (a). If the Commission picks (b) or (c), CANT-DO should be re-worded to match — the documents note the dependency but cannot make the choice.
5. **Path A latency is unverified.** Whether dispatch/CAD can ingest outside reads and match in real time (spec v2 Appendix B question 11) is still an open inquiry. A2/C2 and the CANT-DO real-time language are written to be true under either answer, but if Path A matching turns out to be batch/manual rather than real-time, the hit-latency expectations in OPERATIONS.md §3 need a Commission-set number before go-live.
6. **Nothing here prices Path B.** Consistent with INTERFACE.md (out of scope) — CONTINGENCIES D2 defines the pricing *gate* and the K2 break-even band from the record, but no CJIS cost figures are asserted anywhere in Track B's documents.

No Stoic Opposition branding appears in any Track B document. No camera location is asserted beyond the community-mapped points already in `cameras.yaml`, per-site models are not asserted, and Home Depot / Going Dr ownership is treated as unverified in every document that touches it.
