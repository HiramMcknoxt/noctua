## The headliner: two-factor authentication fails open (Critical)

Four seats converged independently on the same defect and the same forced change; two executed it:

- An enabled human account with **no TOTP enrollment record** authenticates key-only (`_totp_required` requires an entry to exist before it requires a code).
- A **missing or corrupt TOTP store** resolves to an empty mapping — same waiver, for every human account at once.
- A **restore** (the store is excluded from backups by design) silently returns every enrolled human to key-only, with no must_reenroll materialized.
- A file attacker who **deletes one file** gets the same department-wide downgrade with **zero chain evidence**.
- /health does not degrade on any of it.

Around it, a supporting cluster: **no throttle or lockout on second-factor attempts** (300 wrong codes in 0.4 s executed; with a stolen key the factor falls in minutes) — while OPERATIONS.md §10 promises a five-failure lockout that **does not exist in code**; and the **AUTH_FAIL chain cap can be sprayed** to blind the audit trail before a targeted attack (executed). On Path B, the Claude seat notes, these are CJIS compliance failures, not just defects — recorded for the Ben/ACIC lanes, not decided here.

## Consolidated findings (verified status in VERIFICATION-NOTES.md)

1. **Fail-open authentication state** — Critical/High, confirmed four ways. (Above.)
2. **No second-factor throttling; promised lockout absent; AUTH_FAIL cap blinding** — High, executed.
3. **Health fails open** — High/Medium cluster: demo mode invisible to /health (executed), no-sink reports healthy (executed), sink-status exception reads as no-backlog, guard refusals unsurfaced, enrolled_active 0 not a degradation.
4. **Rollback is detected but not fenced** — High: across a checkpoint the mismatch is chained, yet the service keeps serving and extending the truncated history; a consistent (DB, checkpoints) pair rollback is invisible in code — the Clerk's external copy is the only detector and /health never compares the witnessed root. The sink-delivery leg of rollback (Claude F6) is code-consistent and owes a discriminating case.
5. **Checkpoint parsing crashes boot** — Medium, confirmed: a `null` checkpoints line (unguarded `cp.get`) and a malformed checkpoint pubkey (unguarded `base64.b64decode` in `pubkey_fingerprint`) both propagate uncaught through the startup anchor check. One defensive-validation fix at the boundary.
6. **Signature verification is algorithm-label-conditional** — Medium, confirmed: checkpoints (and, per Claude, sink entries) skip signature checks when `sig_alg` is absent or not exactly "ed25519".
7. **RECOVERY_USED is chained outside DBLOCK** — Medium, mechanism confirmed: S11's append inside `_totp_accept` runs on the auth path, which includes the out-of-lock /verify pre-warm — the R4 regression shape, reintroduced by the newest feature.
8. **Existence oracles** — Medium: /verify response codes reconstruct protected hits in real time (executed); POST /hotlist's 403-vs-404 distinguishes protected plates and the probe is unchained (code-consistent).
9. **One ingest identity can fabricate reads at any camera** — Medium, executed. Per-camera ingest identities are the fix direction. Jobs-key heartbeat forgery (any job name, ok:true) is the same identity-binding gap — and, per Claude, makes a jobs key an alerting kill-switch.
10. **Provisioning custody** — Medium cluster: shell enroll-2fa bypasses two-key and is unchained; re-enrollment hands the new secret to the confirming auditor; API keys don't rotate on TOTP change; **chain_auditor cannot present TOTP at all** (the audit scripts send no code — confirmed by grep), so the outside auditor's role is single-factor in practice.
11. **Backup set combines every secret domain unencrypted** — Medium, confirmed from backup.py's own header (DB + config + evidence + audit key + signing private key; docs describe an "encrypted backup" the script does not perform).
12. **Scrubber homoglyph gap** — Medium, from the ARGUS hunt but a Janus finding: `_PLATE_RE` matches ASCII only; homoglyph/full-width encodings pass the chain scrubber. The registry/scrubber work of earlier rounds inherits this.
13. **Suppression-leg residue (DeepSeek F3)** — partially confirmed: the SQL leg honors only the current plate_ref formula; recovery runs on the ingest path only, so stored reads never re-ingested remain an open window. Owes a discriminating case.

Refuted/downgraded, preserved for the record: ChatGPT's concurrent-replay race (SR1-03) did **not** reproduce under the cold seat's execution (1 of 16 authenticated) — downgraded. DeepSeek's keep-alive memo persistence (F5) is neutralized by the HTTP/1.0 default. Hunt 2 (openssl construction) is **settled clean** by four seats — argv lists, no shell, data via temp files; residual Lows: bare `openssl` PATH resolution, a leading-dash path edge, a key-creation chmod window.

## ARGUS — ruled on, unanimously negative as written

All four seats reject SPEC v0.1 as written. Convergent defects: automatic DB-state rollback is destructive and abusable (an attacker who can induce an invariant failure chooses the rollback timing; it bypasses the restore ceremony entirely); a jobs-class key is an alerting kill-switch, not least privilege; the sanitizer is a scrubber with an ASCII-only regex, not a boundary; research-result quarantine is prompt-level; same-host proxy fails the threat model. **v0.2 direction (not commissioned):** read-only advisor holding NO Janus credential; any operational action executed by a deterministic non-LLM policy daemon with a human-edited allowlist; Tier B advisory only; research on separate hardware or cut from the pilot; rollback only as a chained two-key restore ceremony; ARGUS's review log registered in PLATE_DATA_STORES; deterministic alerts on their own unfilterable channel, independent of ARGUS. Build remains gated on Corey's explicit go.

## The 1.2.5 commission (items T1–T9)

Acceptance standard: as for 1.2.3/1.2.4 — verified invariant satisfaction, not passing counts. Every T item carries a discriminating case that FAILS on 1.2.4 and PASSES on 1.2.5 (or a written absence-proof); the full prior battery (builders' suite, round-4/5/6 suites, Q, R, S batteries, load envelope) stays green; Vesper re-runs everything cold from the repo layout before Round 8 is called. The builder works in a new tree `janus125/`, prior trees untouched, no git pushes, HANDOFF.md + run-next.sh per the council handoff protocol, maker's mark per standing rule.

- **T1 Fail-closed authentication state** (the four seats' forced change). Every human-role account owes a valid second factor, enrolled or not: missing/unreadable/empty store, or a human account with no entry, is an authentication failure — never a waiver. Refusals chain a 2FA_NOT_ENROLLED / AUTH_STATE event. /health names every unenrolled human account as a degradation. Restore materializes must_reenroll for all human users when the store is absent (and when it is present but an account is missing). Fresh-install ceremony: the FIRST boot with zero human enrollments is the only unenrolled state allowed, and it is health-red until provisioning completes. TOTP entry-shape validation (malformed entries refuse cleanly, never 500). The demo waiver rides ONLY on S4's server-side install mode — and under T4 that mode is itself a named health degradation. **chain_auditor tooling ships with TOTP support** (compact.py, sentinel_audit.py present codes) so the fail-closed rule doesn't lock out the outside auditor; if a script cannot present a code, its identity is redesigned as a machine identity with fences re-verified, and the choice is documented.
- **T2 Attempt throttling and honest failure evidence.** Per-account second-factor throttling with a real lockout that delivers OPERATIONS.md §10's promise (five failures lock the account; unlock is a supervisor action, chained). Per-target failure counting that the global AUTH_FAIL cap cannot blind: the cap may throttle chain volume but a per-target counter in /health and a chained THROTTLE event preserve the evidence. AUTH_FAIL's username hash becomes keyed (HMAC under the audit key), matching the case-number rule.
- **T3 Anchor-mismatch fence + witness consumption.** A detected anchor mismatch fences data service (the KEY_LOCKED posture family): reads/searches/shares refuse, safety writes (protection orders, ingest) proceed with chained warnings — the R12 fence line applied to rollback. /health surfaces the last external witness root + timestamp and fails closed when the local chain's root at the attested seq disagrees. The rollback-then-append sink case (Claude F6's shape) enters the battery.
- **T4 Health fail-closed sweep.** Unknown ≠ healthy, everywhere: sink status unknown degrades; no-sink-configured is a named degradation (checkpoints-only posture, labeled); demo mode is a named degradation alert_check reports; config guard refusals surface in config_status with the guard message; enrolled_active 0 with human accounts present degrades (subsumed by T1 but stated).
- **T5 Strict signature verification.** Signature verification is never conditional on a declared algorithm label: a checkpoint or sink entry without a verifiable Ed25519 signature is invalid, full stop; unknown/absent sig_alg is a verification failure, chained and health-visible.
- **T6 Defensive checkpoint parsing.** _load_checkpoints/_bad_checkpoints validate entry shape (dict, typed fields, decodable pubkey) at the boundary; malformed entries are bad checkpoints — chained, health-alarmed, never an exception; boot survives a hostile checkpoints file. Cases: the null line, the malformed PEM, a non-dict line, a truncated final line.
- **T7 Identity binding for machine roles.** Per-camera ingest identities (a read is accepted only from the identity bound to its camera); heartbeat writes bound to the presenting identity (a jobs key can report only its own job); /verify and /hotlist response shaping so protected-plate existence is not distinguishable (uniform refusal, chained probes). Frigate redelivery cannot withdraw a PENDING hit by changing the plate (Claude F8's shape gets a case; the withdrawal path requires the original plate or a supervisor action).
- **T8 Provisioning and backup custody.** enroll-2fa honors two-key in the shell path too (or refuses where it cannot) and every provisioning mutation chains; re-enrollment never returns the new secret to the confirmer (delivered to the account holder's enrollment ceremony only); API key rotates when the TOTP secret changes. Backup: the set is encrypted at rest by the script itself, or the key material is split from the data set with the split documented; the docs' "encrypted backup" wording is made true or removed. openssl resolved by pinned path where the platform allows, with the PATH residual documented in CANT-DO.
- **T9 Scrubber and suppression completeness.** The plate scrubber normalizes confusable Unicode (NFKC + a confusables map) before matching, with cases for Cyrillic/full-width homoglyph plates in chain payloads and free-text fields. The DeepSeek F3 discriminating case (stored read, recovered plate-less order, SQL-leg search) enters the battery and the asymmetry closes or is proven absent.

## Pilot pen-test gate clause (synthesized from four seat drafts — for the pilot spec)

> **PILOT PEN-TEST GATE (non-waivable).** Before any camera is energized on the pilot deployment, an independent penetration test of the deployed pilot system — not a design review, not a staging build — must be completed, its findings remediated and re-tested. Scope, at minimum: (a) the application — the Janus/Sentinel HTTP surface, authentication including the full TOTP lifecycle (enrollment, activation, replay, recovery, restore), role and case gates, suppression behavior under historical plate-reference formulas and plate-less orders, Tier 2/3 search, evidence, share and rotation endpoints; (b) the network — camera VLAN isolation, the WireGuard backhaul, localhost binding, the absence of an internet route from the camera network; (c) key custody and recovery, run as live ceremonies — audit key, signing keypair, TOTP store, backups, and a restore-from-backup exercise. The tester has no employment or contracting relationship with the system's authors or the Department. The floor is the free federal services available to the City (CISA Cyber Hygiene, MS-ISAC); the standard is a paid independent application-and-network test under a written scope approved by the City Attorney. No camera goes live and no live plate data is ingested until the report is delivered and every Critical/High finding is remediated and re-tested, or formally accepted in writing by the accountable city official with a named compensating control, the acceptance chained as a SYSTEM entry. The report, the retest, and every disposition go to the Council and the outside auditor before the capture gate opens. This gate survives schedule pressure and is repeated before any expansion and after any change to authentication, key-custody, or restore code.

Applied to the pilot spec when the document freeze lifts, or sooner on Corey's word.
